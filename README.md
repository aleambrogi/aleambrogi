# Alessandro Ambrogi

### Data analysis · Statistical learning · Spatial statistics

I am a Mathematical Engineering MSc graduate from **Politecnico di Milano**, specialising in **Statistical Learning** (110/110). My work focuses on preparing heterogeneous datasets, understanding spatial patterns and building interpretable statistical indicators.

During my thesis experience at **Human Technopole**, in collaboration with **POLIS Lombardia**, I worked on municipal territorial fragility, integrating environmental, sociodemographic and health-related data. I am interested in **data analyst and junior data scientist opportunities**.
